title: Maternity Leave Policy
category: leave
source: sample-hr-policy
Eligible employees may apply for maternity leave by submitting a request to HR with the expected delivery date and supporting medical documents.

The leave request should be submitted as early as possible so that the manager and HR team can plan work coverage.

Employees returning from maternity leave should coordinate with HR and their manager for joining formalities, role continuity, and any approved flexible working arrangements.

title: Reimbursement Policy
category: finance
source: sample-hr-policy
Business expenses are reimbursable when they are approved by the reporting manager and supported by valid invoices or receipts.

Employees should submit reimbursement claims through the approved HR or finance system within the configured claim submission window.

Claims without receipts, approval, or a clear business purpose may be rejected or returned for clarification.
---
title: Attendance Policy
category: attendance
source: sample-hr-policy
Employees are expected to record attendance through the official attendance system every working day.

Late arrivals, early departures, missed punches, or regularization requests should be corrected according to the attendance workflow.

Repeated attendance violations may be reviewed by the manager and HR as part of employee discipline and performance processes.
---
title: Work From Home Policy
category: workplace
source: sample-hr-policy
Work from home may be allowed when the employee's role, project needs, manager approval, and company policy permit remote work.

Employees working from home must remain available during working hours, attend required meetings, and maintain confidentiality of company data.

Managers may revoke or modify work from home arrangements when business requirements, collaboration needs, or performance concerns require office presence.
---
title: Payroll Policy
category: payroll
source: sample-hr-policy
Payroll is processed according to the organization's payroll calendar, cutoff date, and salary credit schedule.

Salary changes, unpaid leave, reimbursements, deductions, and arrears are included only when submitted and approved before the payroll cutoff date.

Employees should contact HR or payroll support if salary components, deductions, or bank account details appear incorrect.
---
title: Notice Period Policy
category: separation
source: sample-hr-policy
Employees resigning from the organization must submit resignation through the approved system or written process.

The applicable notice period depends on employment agreement, grade, role, local law, and company policy.

Release date, notice period recovery, knowledge transfer, asset return, and final settlement are coordinated by the manager, HR, IT, and payroll teams.
